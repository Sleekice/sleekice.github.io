My Story

Lagos shaped me.

I grew up in Lagos, Nigeria — one of Africa's largest, most vibrant, and fastest-moving cities. Lagos doesn't give you the luxury of moving slowly. It taught me to think fast, adapt constantly, and find creative solutions to hard problems with limited resources. Those instincts followed me into engineering.

Minnesota made me a developer.

I came to the United States and earned my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Minnesota State University, Moorhead. It was there that I discovered my passion for systems — not just writing code, but understanding how things connect, scale, and fail. I spent over a year as a Research Assistant alongside faculty researchers, working in high-performance computing, MPI, Python, parallel algorithms, matrix operations, and network graph computations on distributed systems.

That research background gave me something most Android developers don't have: a deep appreciation for performance, concurrency, and what happens when systems get large.

Navy Federal was the proving ground.

As a Digital Engineer II (Senior Android Developer) at Navy Federal Credit Union, I built production Android features used by hundreds of thousands of members in one of the most demanding, regulated environments in American banking. I owned the Account Summary experience, Credit Score integration, Financial Insights, Charged-Off Account experiences, and a WireMock Cloud mock infrastructure that kept the team moving during backend outages.

I worked alongside a 20-person cross-functional Agile team — product owners, UX designers, backend engineers, QA, data analysts — and learned what it really means to ship software in a regulated, production environment where failure affects real people's money.

AI is the next chapter.

I'm currently pursuing my Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence with a specialization in Data Analytics at Indiana Wesleyan University. I'm not just studying AI academically — I'm building the skills to bring AI directly into Android: on-device inference, ML Kit integration, generative AI, intelligent automation, and data-driven mobile experiences.

The intersection of mobile engineering + artificial intelligence is where I'm planting my flag. It's the most exciting place in software right now, and I'm building toward it deliberately.

I'm an owl person.

Outside of code, I have a deep love for owls and birds. There's something I relate to in the owl: it sees clearly in the dark, moves with precision, and acts with patience. That's how I try to approach hard engineering problems. I don't rush — I observe, understand, and then strike accurately.
